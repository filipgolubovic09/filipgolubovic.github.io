About Me

Welcome to my world of basketball, where the passion for the game runs deep! My name is Filip Golubovic. I'm a student-athlete born in Toronto in 2009, and I've been dribbling, shooting, and dreaming on the hardwood for as long as I can remember. Basketball isn't just a sport — it's my life's pursuit.

I proudly represent my prep team at Hodan Nalayeh Secondary School in Vaughan, Ontario, while honing my skills as a point guard with high basketball IQ. I'm highly coachable, with excellent court vision, precise shooting, sharp passing, exceptional footwork, and ball-handling that lets me impact the game in multiple ways. For the 2026 AAU season, I'm repping Toronto Select Basketball 🇨🇦.

Inspired by my mom's Serbian heritage 🇷🇸 and her university-level basketball background, I carry that legacy with pride while forging my own path. My ultimate goal: Earn a Division 1 college scholarship through daily effort and dedication. Thank you for following my journey — I'm committed to excellence and excited to connect with programs!
